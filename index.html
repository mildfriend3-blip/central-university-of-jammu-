About the University

The Central University of Jammu (CUJ) came into existence on August 08, 2011, established under the Central Universities Act, 2009. Located in Bagla (Rahya-Suchani) in the Samba district of Jammu & Kashmir, the university features a sprawling, green campus equipped with modern, state-of-the-art amenities.

It is strictly dedicated to providing high-quality, internationally benchmarked education and fostering cutting-edge research across diverse multidisciplinary domains. With a strong vision aligned with modern educational standards, CUJ actively aims to enrich the academic landscape of the region while contributing to the socio-economic development of the nation. It nurtures technical innovation, encourages critical thinking, and meticulously prepares students for global technological challenges.

Department of CSE

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at CU Jammu is committed to delivering top-tier technical education and fostering high-end innovation in computing technologies. The curriculum thoroughly focuses on the fundamentals of computer programming, networking, data science, and AI, considering its broad and modern scope.

Guided by highly qualified faculty members, the department emphasizes hands-on practical learning through modernized computer laboratories and collaborative real-world projects.
