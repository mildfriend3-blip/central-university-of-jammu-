About the University

Located in the serene environment of Bagla (Rahya-Suchani), Samba district, the Central University of Jammu offers a sprawling, green campus dedicated to comprehensive academic growth.

Prof. Sanjeev Jain Vice Chancellor, CUJ
🏛️

Our Foundation

Established under the Central Universities Act, 2009, the university came into existence on August 08, 2011. It is equipped with state-of-the-art amenities designed to foster an optimal learning environment for students across the nation.

👁️

Our Vision

To be a premier institution that enriches the academic landscape of the region, meticulously preparing students for global technological challenges while contributing significantly to the socio-economic development of India.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide internationally benchmarked education, promote multidisciplinary research, nurture technical innovation, and encourage critical thinking under the leadership of a progressive and experienced administration.

Department of CSE

Pioneering technical education and high-end innovation in modern computing technologies.

The curriculum is strictly aligned with industry standards, thoroughly focusing on computer programming, networking, data science, Artificial Intelligence, and cybersecurity. Students are immersed in hands-on practical learning through collaborative real-world projects.
