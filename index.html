About the University

Our Foundation

Established under the Central Universities Act, 2009, the Central University of Jammu (CUJ) came into existence on August 08, 2011. Located in Bagla (Rahya-Suchani) in the Samba district, the campus offers a sprawling, green environment equipped with state-of-the-art amenities.

Our Vision

Dedicated to providing internationally benchmarked education and fostering cutting-edge research. CUJ actively aims to enrich the academic landscape, nurture technical innovation, encourage critical thinking, and prepare students for global technological challenges.

Department of CSE

Committed to delivering top-tier technical education and fostering high-end innovation in computing technologies.

The curriculum thoroughly focuses on the fundamentals of computer programming, networking, data science, and AI. Guided by highly qualified faculty, the department emphasizes hands-on practical learning through modernized laboratories and real-world projects.
